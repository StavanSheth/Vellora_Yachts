We sell yachts. But we’re far more than sales people. We’re advisors. Experts. Passionate people, with an unrivalled understanding of the intricacies of our industry. And for us, yacht sales means more than selling quickly and negotiating an attractive price. We consider your unique requirements at every single stage of the process, from initial enquiry, to pricing, inclusions, offer acceptance, closing timeframe and location. Carefully tailoring our sales strategy to your best interests.

Selling Your Superyacht?

Here's what to know.

Why should I use a yacht broker?

While you don't have to use a broker, using one that has the experience and the industry reputation to recommend your yacht with credibility, and who will also dedicate continued time and energy to selling your yacht will certainly help.

Meet our yacht sales team
What is a Yacht Broker?

A yacht broker will negotiate on your behalf to achieve the best price for your yacht. Once a sale is agreed, the broker collates all documentation and completes the transfer of ownership. We coordinate the exchange of contracts, arranging and monitoring all payments on your behalf.

Read our guide to selling a superyacht
How can I market my yacht to potential buyers?

When selling yachts with Velora, you can expect us to use insight, market intelligence, and data to competitively price your yacht. Our in-house marketing team will produce a bespoke marketing and promotion strategy to ensure your yacht receives maximum visibility.

Learn more about our marketing team
What yachts have Velora sold previously?

We understand your yacht is a prized possession. Whatever the reason for selling, our team will work hard to deliver a swift, discreet sale, securing the best price possible. Our clients trust us to act on their behalf to locate and close the sale.
